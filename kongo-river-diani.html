Description

Discover the beauty of Kongo River with Kachumbari Travel Tours’ Sunset Boat Ride, a must-do experience in Diani Beach, Kenya. As the sun sets, the river glows with golden hues, creating a picture-perfect scene for nature lovers, couples, and adventure seekers. Cruise through the mangrove forests, spot exotic birds, and enjoy the tranquil waters as our expert guides share insights into the rich Swahili heritage, local wildlife, and unique ecosystem. This top-rated sunset boat safari offers the perfect blend of relaxation, romance, and exploration, making it a highlight of your Kenyan coastal getaway.

Book your Kongo River sunset boat tour today and witness one of Kenya’s most breathtaking natural wonders. Whether you're capturing stunning Instagram photos, unwinding in nature’s serenity, or enjoying a romantic evening cruise, this experience is tailored for unforgettable memories. With affordable pricing, expert guides, and a commitment to safety, Kachumbari Travel Tours ensures a seamless and highly-rated travel experience. Reserve your sunset boat ride now and explore the magic of Diani Beach’s best river tour!

Itinerary
01
Pick-Up & Transfer

From 3:30 PM

Guests are picked up from hotels, resorts, or designated locations in Diani Beach, Ukunda, or SGR.
Enjoy a comfortable ride to the Kongo River departure point.

02
Arrival & Check-in

From 4:00 PM

Meet our friendly Kachumbari Travel Tours team and receive a safety briefing.
Board the boat and get ready for an unforgettable sunset cruise.

03
Scenic River Cruise

From 4:30 PM

Glide through the lush mangrove forests of Kongo River.
Spot exotic birds, local wildlife, and breathtaking natural scenery.
Learn about the Swahili culture and history of Kongo River from our expert guides.

04
Sunset Experience

From 5:45 PM

Witness a stunning sunset over Kongo River, with golden reflections on the water.
Capture Instagram-worthy moments of the beautiful scenery.
Enjoy a peaceful ride as the boat gently drifts through the calm waters.

05
Return & Drop-Off

From 6:30 PM

Begin the journey back to the dock and disembark.
Transfer back to your hotel or preferred drop-off point in Diani Beach, Ukunda, or SGR.

Frequently Asked Questions

The boat ride lasts approximately 2 hours, and if you choose the bonfire experience, the total duration is about 4 hours.

The tour typically starts at 4:00 PM and ends around 6:30 PM, while the bonfire experience extends until 9:30 PM.

To secure your spot on the Kongo River Boat Ride, you can visit Kachumbari Travel Tours’ website or contact us directly via phone:+254745 162 782. It’s advisable to book in advance due to limited availability.

We recommend bringing:
✔️ A camera or phone for stunning sunset photos
✔️ Comfortable clothing and sandals
✔️ A light jacket (it can get chilly in the evening)
✔️ Bug spray (optional but recommended)

Yes! We can provide refreshments, snacks, and even a BBQ dinner at the bonfire upon request.
