Description

Embark on a captivating journey with Kachumbari Travel Tours to Tsavo East National Park, one of Kenya’s most iconic and wildlife-rich destinations. Our Tsavo East Safari tour offers an unforgettable adventure through diverse landscapes, from vast savannahs to scenic riverbanks, where you’ll witness an extraordinary array of wildlife including elephants, lions, cheetahs, and over 500 bird species. With our expert guides, you'll explore the famous Yatta Plateau and Mudanda Rock, learning about the park's rich history, unique ecosystems, and its conservation efforts. Whether you're a seasoned traveler or a first-time visitor, Kachumbari provides an immersive and enriching experience that brings the magic of Tsavo East to life.

Designed to cater to both solo travelers and families, Kachumbari Travel Tours offers customized safari packages that can be tailored to suit your preferences, ensuring that your Tsavo East experience is nothing short of spectacular. From comfortable accommodations to convenient transport options, we handle all the details so you can focus on the wonders of the wild. Explore the Tsavo East Safari at your own pace with a variety of packages, whether you’re after a day trip, a weekend getaway, or a longer stay. Book your tour today and discover why Kachumbari Travel Tours is the go-to choice for authentic safaris in Kenya, where adventure meets comfort in the heart of Tsavo East.

Highlights

Morning: Guests arrive at Tsavo East via Tsavo Gate. Upon entry, you'll be greeted with the beauty of Tsavo's wilderness and its wildlife.

Drive to Boma Simba Safari Lodge: Your first stop is the renowned Boma Simba Safari Lodge, located in a prime area within the park. The scenic drive gives guests their first views of the park's diverse landscapes.

Wildlife Watching: This is a great time to spot the famous “Maneless Lions of Tsavo,” as well as cheetahs, leopards, and other wildlife that roam the park.

Itinerary
01
Day 1 - Pickup from Hotel in Diani

From 7:30am

Enjoy an early breakfast and get ready for pickup from your hotel in Diani by the Kachumari Travel Tours driver. The vehicle is a comfortable safari minivan or 4x4 depending on the group size.

02
Drive to Tsavo East National Park

6:30 AM – 9:00 AM

Start the journey to Tsavo East National Park, located about 3 hours away from Diani. On the way, you'll pass through scenic landscapes with beautiful views of the coast, lush forests, and local villages.
Enjoy a smooth drive with a brief stop along the way for a short stretch or refreshment.

03
Arrival and Morning Game Drive

9:00 AM – 12:00 PM

Upon arrival at Tsavo East, you will begin your morning game drive. The park is famous for its large elephant herds, so you’re likely to spot these majestic creatures early on.
The park is also home to other wildlife such as lions, giraffes, zebras, cheetahs, and various bird species.
Enjoy the panoramic views of the park, especially the Mzima Springs area, which is famous for its freshwater springs and wildlife sightings.

04
Lunch at Lodge/Camp

12:00 PM – 1:00 PM

Head to your pre-arranged lodge or camp within the park. A delicious buffet or packed lunch will be served depending on the tour package.
Relax and enjoy the local dishes. There might be time for a short rest before continuing with the activities.

05
Afternoon Game Drive

1:00 PM – 3:00 PM

After lunch, embark on another exciting game drive to explore more areas of Tsavo East.
Visit key spots such as the Aruba Dam, a watering hole often frequented by animals. This is an excellent spot for wildlife photography.
You may also stop by the iconic Yatta Plateau, which offers stunning panoramic views of the park.

06
Check-in and Relaxation at the Lodge/Camp

3:00 PM – 4:00 PM

Check-in to your accommodation, where you can unwind and refresh.
If staying at a lodge, enjoy a swim or relax with a drink by the pool as you take in the surroundings.

07
Evening Game Drive

4:00 PM – 6:30 PM

Take another short evening game drive. Tsavo East’s animals are often more active during the cooler parts of the day, so you might encounter more wildlife.
Watch as the sun begins to set, casting beautiful colors across the savannah, perfect for a serene moment of reflection or photography.

08
Dinner at the Lodge/Camp

7:00 PM

Enjoy a sumptuous dinner at the lodge or camp. Savor local Kenyan cuisine, often including freshly made meats, vegetables, rice, and salads.
Depending on your lodge or camp, you might enjoy entertainment such as traditional music or dancing.

09
DAY 2: Early Morning Game Drive

5:30 AM – 6:30 AM

Wake up early for an optional morning game drive. Tsavo East offers some of the best sightings of wildlife during the early hours when animals are most active.
Your guide will take you to some of the park's best spots for animal sightings, including the Voi River and its surrounding bush areas.

10
DAY 2: Breakfast at the Lodge/Camp

6:30 AM – 7:30 AM

Return to your lodge or camp for a filling breakfast, which will prepare you for the journey back to Diani.

11
DAY 2: Early Morning Game Drive

5:30 AM – 6:30 AM

Wake up early for an optional morning game drive. Tsavo East offers some of the best sightings of wildlife during the early hours when animals are most active.
Your guide will take you to some of the park's best spots for animal sightings, including the Voi River and its surrounding bush areas.

12
DAY 2: Final Game Drive and Explore Tsavo East

7:30 AM – 9:30 AM

Before departing, you can enjoy one last short game drive around the park, or if time permits, a visit to the famous Mzima Springs, a natural spring where you can see hippos and crocodiles in the crystal-clear water.

13
Drive Back to Diani

9:30 AM – 12:00 PM

Start your journey back to Diani. The scenic drive back offers more chances to enjoy the landscape and relax after your adventure.
There may be a stop along the way for a coffee break or a quick snack.
Expect to arrive back in Diani around lunchtime, where you will be dropped off at your hotel or designated location.

At Kachumbari Travel Tours, we understand that plans may change, and we strive to be as accommodating as possible. Please review our cancellation policy to ensure a smooth process in case of cancellations.

1. Cancellation by the Traveler

1. More than 30 days before the tour date: A full refund will be issued, minus a 10% processing fee.
2. 15 to 30 days before the tour date: 50% of the total booking fee will be refunded.
3. Less than 15 days before the tour date: No refund will be issued.

2. Cancellation by Kachumbari Travel Tours

In the event that Kachumbari Travel Tours must cancel a tour due to unforeseen circumstances (e.g., safety concerns, weather conditions, or other unavoidable events), a full refund will be issued to the traveler, or the traveler may choose to reschedule the tour for a later date.

3. Rescheduling Policy

1. Rescheduling: If you wish to reschedule your tour, please notify us at least 7 days before the original departure date. A rescheduling fee of 10% of the total tour cost may apply.
2. Same-Day Rescheduling: If rescheduling occurs within 24 hours of the tour date, a fee of 20% will apply.

5. Force Majeure

In case of force majeure events (natural disasters, strikes, pandemics, etc.), Kachumbari Travel Tours is not liable for any cancellations or delays. Travelers will be offered an alternative date or tour option, or a full refund, depending on the circumstances.
