Description

Experience an unforgettable Shimba Hills Safari with Kachumbari Travel Tours, the #1-rated safari from Mombasa and Diani Beach. Just 45 km from Mombasa, Shimba Hills National Reserve is a hidden paradise filled with majestic elephants, the rare sable antelope, giraffes, leopards, and over 200 bird species. Enjoy a thrilling game drive, spotting incredible wildlife in the lush tropical forest, followed by a guided hike to Sheldrick Falls, one of Kenya’s most breathtaking waterfalls. This top-rated safari near Mombasa offers the perfect blend of adventure, relaxation, and stunning scenery, making it a must-visit destination for nature lovers and photographers.

Book your Shimba Hills Tour today with Kachumbari Travel Tours, your trusted Mombasa safari expert. Our all-inclusive package includes hotel pickup and drop-off, professional guides, park entry fees, and a comfortable game drive through one of Kenya’s most scenic reserves. Marvel at panoramic views of the coastline, enjoy a delicious lunch, and create lifelong memories in this wildlife haven near Diani Beach. Whether you're on a family vacation, honeymoon, or solo adventure, this safari promises an authentic Kenyan wildlife experience.

Highlights

Scenic Game Drive – Explore Shimba Hills National Reserve, home to elephants, rare sable antelopes, giraffes, buffaloes, leopards, and over 200 bird species.

Sheldrick Falls Nature Walk – Take a guided hike through the tropical forest to witness the breathtaking 25-meter waterfall, perfect for relaxation and photography.

Panoramic Viewpoints – Capture stunning coastal views, rolling green hills, and lush landscapes, offering some of the best scenic spots near Mombasa and Diani Beach.

Itinerary
01
Hotel Pickup

From 7:30am

✅ Pick up from Mombasa, Diani Beach, Ukunda, or Tiwi.
✅ Drive towards Shimba Hills National Reserve, enjoying scenic coastal views along the way.

02
Arrival & Game Drive

From 9:00am

✅ Enter Shimba Hills National Reserve and begin an exciting game drive.
✅Spot elephants, rare sable antelopes, giraffes, buffaloes, leopards, and a variety of bird species.
✅Stop at viewpoints for breathtaking panoramas of the Indian Ocean and lush green hills.

03
Sheldrick Falls Nature Walk

At 11:30am

✅ Embark on a guided hike to Sheldrick Falls, a stunning 25-meter waterfall hidden in the forest.
✅ Enjoy the cool mist, relax by the falls, and capture stunning photos.

04
Lunch Break

At 1:00pm

✅ Enjoy a delicious meal at a scenic picnic spot or a local restaurant .
✅ Take in the beautiful views of the rolling hills and coastal plains.

05
Afternoon Game Drive & Scenic Viewpoints

At 2:30pm

✅ Continue exploring the reserve’s diverse landscapes and wildlife
✅ Visit the Marere Viewpoint, offering breathtaking views of the Kenyan coast.

06
Depart for Mombasa/Diani

At 4:00 PM

✅ Begin the journey back, making a scenic stop at a viewpoint for final photos.

07
Drop-off at Your Hotel

At 6:00pm

Arrive at your hotel or residence, ending an unforgettable Shimba Hills Safari experience!

We recommend bringing comfortable clothing, walking shoes, a hat, sunglasses, sunscreen, a camera, and binoculars for better wildlife viewing.

The walk to Sheldrick Falls takes about 30-40 minutes and is moderate in difficulty. It is suitable for most visitors, but comfortable shoes are recommended.

You may spot elephants, rare sable antelopes, giraffes, leopards, buffaloes, warthogs, and various bird species during the game drive.
