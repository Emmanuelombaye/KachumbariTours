Description

Join Kachumbari Travel Tours for an exciting and immersive Mombasa City Tour, where history, culture, and adventure come together. Explore the famous Fort Jesus, a UNESCO World Heritage Site that tells the story of Mombasa’s rich past. Walk through the charming Old Town, where Swahili, Arab, and European influences blend seamlessly in its architecture and markets. Capture stunning photos at the Mombasa Tusks, a landmark symbolizing the city’s heritage. As part of the experience, indulge in delicious Swahili cuisine, interact with locals, and soak in the vibrant coastal atmosphere.

This Mombasa sightseeing tour goes beyond just visiting landmarks—it’s about creating unforgettable memories. Enjoy a visit to Haller Park, home to giraffes, crocodiles, and giant tortoises, or relax on a scenic dhow ride along the beautiful Indian Ocean. With comfortable transportation, expert guides, and a well-planned itinerary, everything is taken care of, so you can enjoy a stress-free and enriching adventure. Don’t just visit Mombasa—experience it with Kachumbari Travel Tours! Book your tour today and let us show you the best of Kenya’s coastal paradise.

Itinerary
01
Pick-up & Welcome Briefing

From 7:30am

1. Pick-up from your hotel, residence, or designated meeting point in Mombasa, Diani, or Nyali.
2. Warm welcome and introduction by our expert guide, outlining the day’s itinerary.

02
Fort Jesus & Old Town Exploration

From 8:00am

1. Tour Fort Jesus, a UNESCO World Heritage Site rich in history, dating back to the 16th century.
2. Walk through Mombasa Old Town, famous for its ancient Swahili architecture, vibrant markets, and cultural heritage.
3. Visit Portuguese Chapel and discover Mombasa’s colonial past.

03
Mombasa Tusks & Uhuru Gardens

At 10:00am

1. Stop by the iconic Mombasa Tusks, a symbol of the city’s history and a great photo spot.
2. Relax at Uhuru Gardens, a serene park with historical significance.

04
Spice Market & Local Shopping Experience

At 11:00am

1. Visit the bustling Mackinnon Market, known for its variety of spices, fresh produce, and handmade crafts.
2. Optional souvenir shopping at Bombolulu Workshops (a social enterprise supporting local artisans).

05
Lunch at a Swahili Restaurant

At 12:30am

Enjoy a delicious Swahili cuisine lunch at a top-rated local restaurant, featuring dishes like pilau, biryani, grilled seafood, and fresh coconut juice.

06
Haller Park Nature & Wildlife Tour

At 2:00pm

1. Explore Haller Park, home to rescued wildlife such as giraffes, hippos, crocodiles, tortoises, and monkeys.
2. Get a chance to feed the giraffes and enjoy a guided nature walk.

07
Dhow Ride & Coastal Views

At 3:30pm

1. Experience a traditional dhow boat ride along the Mombasa coastline, offering stunning views of the Indian Ocean and Mombasa skyline. 2. Enjoy the cool ocean breeze and a chance to spot marine life.

08
Relaxation & Beach Walk

At 4:30pm

1. Unwind with a relaxing beach walk along the scenic Nyali or Bamburi Beach, soaking in the golden sunset.
2. Optional stop at a beachfront café for fresh coconut water or a cocktail.

09
Depart from Wasini Island

At 5:00am

After enjoying your day filled with adventure and cultural experiences, depart from Wasini Island back to Shimoni jetty.

10
Drop-off & End of Tour

6:00pm

1. Safe drop-off at your hotel, residence, or preferred location in Mombasa, Diani, or Nyali.
2. Farewell from our team with an option to book another exciting tour with Kachumbari Travel Tours!
