🚀 Join Our Team at Kachumbari Travel Tours

Discover exciting career opportunities in the travel and tourism industry. If you're passionate about hospitality, adventure, and customer service — we want to hear from you!

🔹 Customer Service Representative

Provide outstanding support to clients before, during, and after their travel experience. Must be enthusiastic, fluent in English and Swahili, and possess strong communication skills.

🔹 Tour Guide (Coastal Excursions)

Lead and educate travelers on exciting tours including Wasini, Shimba Hills, and Mombasa. Knowledge of local culture and sites is a must. French or German is a plus!

Support online visibility and social media content creation. Great opportunity for students or graduates looking to build a portfolio in tourism marketing.
