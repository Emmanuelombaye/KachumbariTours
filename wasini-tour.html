Description

Are you ready for an unforgettable adventure that combines breathtaking scenery, vibrant culture, and luxurious comfort? Join us on our exclusive Wasini Island tour, where we take you on a journey like no other. As the only tour provider offering live music entertainment aboard our boats, we ensure that your experience is filled with joy and rhythm as you glide through the stunning waters. Our luxury vans provide a comfortable ride to and from the island, allowing you to relax and soak in the beautiful landscapes along the way. With expert tour guides leading the way, you’ll gain insights into the rich history and ecology of this enchanting destination.

But that’s not all! We believe that every moment of your journey should be delightful, which is why we offer complimentary refreshments throughout your trip. Whether you’re enjoying a cold drink while basking in the sun or savoring local delicacies during our stops, your satisfaction is our priority. Don’t miss out on this unique opportunity to explore Wasini Island in style and comfort. Book your tour today with Kachumbari Travel Tours and create memories that will last a lifetime!

Highlights

Dolphin Watching: Experience the excitement of observing bottlenose dolphins as they leap and frolic in their natural environment during our adventure cruise.

Entertainment on Boat Ride: Enjoy a fully narrated cruise led by knowledgeable naturalists who share insights about marine wildlife, enriching your overall experience.

Snorkeling: Immerse yourself in the crystal-clear waters for an unforgettable snorkeling experience, where you can explore a vibrant underwater ecosystem.

Itinerary
01
Pickup from Hotels

From 7:30am

Begin your day with an early morning pickup from your hotel located in the north or south coast of Mombasa.

02
Depart for Shimoni Pier

From 8:00am

Continue the journey to Shimoni Pier, which is approximately 50 kilometers away. Enjoy the scenic views along the way.

03
Arrive at Shimoni Jetty

At 9:30am

Upon arrival at Shimoni Jetty, you will meet your experienced dive team and receive a brief introduction about the trip and available options.

04
Sailing and Snorkeling at Kisite Marine Park

At 10:00am

Board an authentic Arabic Dhow and set sail towards Kisite Marine Park. During this time, you can enjoy snorkeling in one of the best marine parks in Kenya, where you may encounter dolphins, turtles, and a variety of colorful fish.

05
Dolphin Watching and Swimming

At 10:30am

If conditions permit, take part in swimming with dolphins. This unique experience allows you to interact with these playful creatures in their natural habitat.

06
Sail to Wasini Island for Lunch

At 2:30pm

After snorkeling, sail to Wasini Island where a delicious lunch awaits you. The meal will be served by local Swahili ladies and includes options like white wine and beer.

07
Cultural Tour of Wasini Island

At 3:30pm

Post-lunch, embark on a guided tour of Wasini Island. Learn about the history and culture of the Swahili people who inhabit the island.

08
Coral Garden Walk

At 4:30pm

Take a leisurely walk through the coral gardens near Wasini Island. This is an opportunity to appreciate the rich marine biodiversity that thrives in this area

09
Depart from Wasini Island

At 5:00am

After enjoying your day filled with adventure and cultural experiences, depart from Wasini Island back to Shimoni jetty.

10
Return Journey to Accommodation

5:00pm

Finally, enjoy a relaxing drive back to your accommodation in Mombasa or Diani, concluding your memorable day trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

The duration of a typical Wasini Dolphin Tour can vary but generally lasts between 5 to 8 hours. This includes travel time from nearby resorts or hotels to Shimoni, as well as time spent on the water observing dolphins and snorkeling.

While dolphin sightings are common, they cannot be guaranteed due to their unpredictable nature. However, Kachumbari Travel Tours strives to provide an enjoyable experience regardless of dolphin sightings by offering other engaging activities like snorkeling and island exploration.

To secure your spot on the Wasini Dolphin Tour, you can visit Kachumbari Travel Tours’ website or contact us directly via phone:+254745 162 782. It’s advisable to book in advance due to limited availability.

The Wasini Dolphin Tour is an exciting day trip that takes visitors to Wasini Island and Kisite Mpunguti Marine Park, where they can experience the beauty of marine life, including playful dolphins, vibrant coral reefs, Live Music Entertainment On Boat and other aquatic treasures. This tour offers a unique opportunity to explore the rich biodiversity of the region while enjoying activities such as snorkeling and boat rides.

There is generally no strict age limit for participants on the Wasini Dolphin Tour, making it suitable for families. However, children should be accompanied by adults at all times during activities like snorkeling or swimming.
