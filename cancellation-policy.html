Cancellation Policy

At Kachumbari Travel Tours, we understand that plans may change unexpectedly. To provide clarity and fairness to our customers, we have outlined our cancellation policy below.


1. Cancellation Policy for Day Trips & Short Excursions:
  • 🔷 More than 7 days before departure: Full refund minus a 10% processing fee.
  • 🔷 3 to 6 days before departure: 50% refund of the total booking amount.
  • 🔷 Less than 48 hours before departure: No refund.
  • 🔷 No-shows: No refund will be provided.
  • 🔷 Late arrivals: Guests arriving late for departure will not be eligible for a refund or rescheduling.

    • 2. Cancellation Policy for Multi-Day Excursions & Packages:

    • 🔷 More than 14 days before departure: Full refund minus a 10% processing fee.

    • 🔷 7 to 13 days before departure: 50% refund of the total booking amount.

    • 🔷 Less than 7 days before departure: No refund.

    • 🔷 No-shows or early departures: No refund will be issued.

      • 3. Cancellation Policy for Private & Custom Tours:

      • 🔷 More than 21 days before departure: Full refund minus a 10% processing fee.

      • 🔷 10 to 20 days before departure: 50% refund of the total booking amount.

      • 🔷 Less than 10 days before departure: No refund.

      • 🔷 Changes to itinerary: Requests for rescheduling are subject to availability and may incur additional charges.

        • 4. Cancellation Policy for SGR & Airport Transfers:

        • 🔷 More than 48 hours before the scheduled transfer: Full refund minus a 10% processing fee.

        • 🔷 24 to 48 hours before the scheduled transfer: 50% refund of the total booking amount.

        • 🔷 Less than 24 hours before the scheduled transfer: No refund.

        • 🔷 No-shows: No refund will be issued.

        • 🔷 Delayed flights/trains: Customers must inform us in advance of any delays. If rescheduling is possible, additional charges may apply.

        • 🔷 Missed Van for SGR Transfer: Customers who arrive late and miss our van will not be eligible for a refund. Rescheduling may be possible at an additional cost, subject to availability.

          • 5. Cancellation Policy for Car Hire Services:

          • 🔷 More than 72 hours before pick-up time: Full refund minus a 10% processing fee.

          • 🔷 24 to 72 hours before pick-up time: 50% refund of the total booking amount.

          • 🔷 Less than 24 hours before pick-up time: No refund.

          • 🔷 No-shows: No refund will be issued.

          • 🔷 Early returns: No refund will be provided for unused rental days.

            • 6. Cancellation Policy for Tour Bookings:

            • 🔷 More than 14 days before the scheduled tour: Full refund minus a 10% processing fee.

            • 🔷 7 to 13 days before the scheduled tour: 50% refund of the total booking amount.

            • 🔷 Less than 7 days before the scheduled tour: No refund.

            • 🔷 No-shows or late arrivals: No refund will be issued.

              • 7. Refund Processing:

              • 🔷 Refunds will be processed within 14 business days from the date of cancellation.

              • 🔷 Refunds will be issued through the original mode of payment.

              • 🔷 Bank transfer fees or transaction charges may apply and will be deducted from the refunded amount.

                • 7. Refund Processing:

                • 🔷 Refunds will be processed within 14 business days from the date of cancellation.

                • 🔷 Refunds will be issued through the original mode of payment.

                • 🔷 Bank transfer fees or transaction charges may apply and will be deducted from the refunded amount.

                  • 8. Force Majeure & Exceptional Circumstances

                  • Kachumbari Travel Tours reserves the right to cancel any tour or service due to unforeseen circumstances such as natural disasters, political instability, government restrictions, or pandemics. In such cases, customers may be eligible for a full or partial refund, travel credit, or rescheduling options.

                    • 9. Changes & Amendments to Bookings:

                    • 🔷 Changes to a booking (such as date changes) may be accommodated subject to availability and may be subject to additional fees.

                    • 🔷 All change requests must be made in writing via email or phone at least 7 days prior to the scheduled departure.

                      • 10. Third-Party Service Providers

                      • For bookings involving third-party providers (such as airlines, hotels, or transport companies), their individual cancellation policies will apply in addition to Kachumbari Travel Tours' policies. Any refund requests will be subject to their terms.
